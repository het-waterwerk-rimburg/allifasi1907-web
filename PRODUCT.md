## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output). Chosen by the user over plain HTML + Tailwind and plain HTML/CSS.

## Users

Mostly holidaymakers on Rhodes, often staying around Lindos, Pefkos and Kalathos. They look for a place to eat on their phone, usually the same day or a few days ahead. They want to know what Alli Fasi is, see the food and the place, read the menu with prices, and reserve a table. Some locals and Greek speakers phone the Greek line.

## Product Purpose

The website for ALLI FASI 1907, a family-run traditional Greek and Rhodian meze restaurant and cafe bar in Kalathos, Rhodes. It must explain the restaurant in 5–10 seconds and turn visits into table reservations. The menu page is equally critical.

## Positioning

A family place since 1907. It started as a village coffee shop (kafeneio) and is now a meze restaurant. It serves family recipes ("Father's Speciality", "Mama's Specialities") and local dishes such as Fried Shrimps from Lindos, Spetsofai, Kolios cured 2 years, and Cuttlefish with Orzo in squid ink. It is 10 minutes from Lindos and 10 minutes from Pefkos.

## Product Principles

1. A reservation is always one tap away: in the header, by phone, and on WhatsApp.
2. The menu is real and complete. Read it on a phone in the evening light; never hide prices.
3. Heritage is shown with restraint: the family, 1907 and Rhodian recipes, without folklore kitsch.
4. Say only what is true. Missing facts stay missing until the owners provide them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visitors are international, and the site is used on phones outdoors and at night. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, large tap targets, and full keyboard use.
