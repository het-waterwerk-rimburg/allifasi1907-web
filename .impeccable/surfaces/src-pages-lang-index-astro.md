## Scope

The whole ALLI FASI 1907 marketing site:
- Home, Menu, Our Story, Restaurant, Rhodian Cuisine, Reviews, and Reservations.
- Languages: EN, EL, DE, FR.
- Visitor mode: Persuade. The first job is to book a table; the menu page is the second.

## Audience and action

- **Who:** holidaymakers near Lindos, Pefkos and Kalathos, on a phone, in the evening.
- **What they need:** to understand what Alli Fasi is, see the food and the terrace, read real prices, and reserve.
- **How they reserve:** WhatsApp (a prefilled form), a phone call, or email.

## Direction contract

THESIS:
- The restaurant presents itself the way a 1907 Rhodian grocer labelled his best olive oil tin: framed, sealed, dated and proud.
- It refuses the category default of a full-bleed food photo with a dark overlay and a centred script headline.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Colour fields:** solid Aegean-teal "tin" fields alternate with cream "label paper" panels.
- **Frames:**
  - Ancient-gold double-rule frames with notched (cut) corners.
  - Meander bands as the tin's printed border.
  - An oval gold cartouche/seal reading ALLI FASI · KALATHOS · 1907.
- **Type:** Cinzel capitals for the display voice, with GFS Didot covering Greek, and Plus Jakarta Sans for the body, with Commissioner covering Greek.
- **Menu:** dotted price leaders, as on a price list.
- **Photos:** sit in label windows framed by gold.
- **Buttons:** olive tabs with a gold keyline.
- **No:** glass, gradients, or emoji.

STORY:
1. The visitor sees a proud, real family house since 1907.
2. They see the actual meze and terrace.
3. They read the full, honest menu.
4. They reserve in one tap, by WhatsApp or by phone.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- The page opens on a teal tin field, edged top and bottom by a gold meander band.
- **Left, about 6 columns:**
  - the H1 in Cinzel cream, about 64px on desktop
  - the subtitle
  - the olive "Reserve a table" button and the outline "View menu" button
  - a pin icon with "Kalathos, Rhodes · 10 min from Lindos"
- **Right, about 6 columns:**
  - the terrace-and-moon photo in a notched gold double frame
  - the oval 1907 seal overlapping its lower-left corner
- **Header:** teal, with the transparent logo mark, the nav, the language switch and the Reserve tab.

FORM:
- The Olive Oil Tin Label (lithographed Greek grocery tins and labels, c. 1900).
- It was #1 on the grounded list, chosen by the user as the pick over the assigned "Lindian Plate Wall".
- Seed key: 702eeb4f.
- Signature interaction: on load, the hero frame's gold rules draw themselves in and then the seal stamps once. It is static under reduced motion.
- Motion grammar: short fade/rise reveals on section entry; a gold keyline on menu-row and card hover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
